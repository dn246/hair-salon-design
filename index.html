Our Story

The Story of 22 Pell

22 Pell Hair Salon has been part of the Chinatown streetscape for over a decade, built on precision cuts, honest color work, and a team that knows their regulars by name.
22 Pell Hair Salon 扎根唐人街十余年,凭借精准的剪裁、诚信的染发工艺,以及一支熟悉每位常客的团队,赢得街坊的信赖。

No overbooking, no rushed color jobs — just skilled stylists, quality products, and a shop that's become a fixture on Pell Street.
不超额预约,不敷衍染发——只有经验丰富的发型师、优质的产品,以及这家已成为佩尔街地标的理发店。
